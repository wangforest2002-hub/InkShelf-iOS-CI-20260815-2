## Koko character base

The bundled Koko 3D character is adapted from `Sendagaya_Shino.vrm`, one of
pixiv Inc.'s VRoid Studio beta sample avatars. The source copy used by the
asset build pipeline is mirrored in
[madjin/vrm-samples](https://github.com/madjin/vrm-samples), and pixiv's
[official sample-model notice](https://vroid.pixiv.help/hc/en-us/articles/4402614652569-Do-VRoid-Studio-s-sample-models-come-with-conditions-of-use)
identifies the beta sample avatars as CC0. The base was converted, renamed and
optimized for InkShelf; no attribution is required by CC0, but this notice is
kept so the asset provenance remains auditable.
